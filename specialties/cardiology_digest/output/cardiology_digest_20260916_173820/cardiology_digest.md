### Intracoronary Imaging-Guided Percutaneous Coronary Intervention: An Updated Meta-Analysis of Randomized Trials.
- **Journal**: J Am Coll Cardiol (2026)
- **Score**: 9/10 | **Subgroup**: Interventional Cardiology
- **Bottom Line**: This comprehensive meta-analysis of 28 RCTs (>24,000 patients) demonstrates that intracoronary imaging-guided PCI significantly reduces hard outcomes including all-cause mortality, cardiac death, MI, and stent thrombosis compared with angiography alone. These findings strongly reinforce guideline recommendations for the routine adoption of intravascular imaging (IVUS/OCT) to optimize PCI outcomes.
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42747372/
